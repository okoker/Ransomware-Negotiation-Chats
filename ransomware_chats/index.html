Ransomware Negotiation Chats

Archive of ransomware negotiations organized by threat actor groups.

Below are transcripts of ransomwhere negotiations with various groups. They may be educational in understanding what works and what doesnt with the various different groups, it might also shed some light into their mindset or origins.


Original corpus was/is collected by @DarkWebInformer (valery(at)proton.me) in a json format at https://github.com/DarkWebInformer/Ransomchats/.


This repo aims to make it easier for manual human consumption.
